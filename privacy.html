01 Introduction

LI Corporation ("we," "us," "our," or "the Company") operates the FeeSlash platform (the "Service"), providing non-custodial TRON blockchain services including USDT ↔ TRX swap functionality and energy rental.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data transparently. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

By accessing or using our Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our Service.

02 Information We Collect

Wallet & Transaction Data

  • Public wallet addresses (TRON network addresses you connect)
  • Transaction hashes and on-chain activity related to your use of the Service
  • Smart contract approvals granted to our platform

We never collect or store your private keys, seed phrases, or mnemonics. FeeSlash is fully non-custodial.

Technical Data

  • IP address (anonymized after 30 days)
  • Browser type, version, and language settings
  • Device type (mobile/desktop) and operating system
  • Referrer URL and referral codes
  • Session identifiers and timestamps

Usage Analytics

  • Pages visited and features used
  • Interaction events (clicks, swaps attempted)
  • Error logs and performance metrics

Communication Data

  • Information you provide via Telegram support channels
  • Messages, inquiries, and feedback you submit

03 How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Service Provision: To facilitate USDT/TRX swaps, energy rental, and related features
  • Security: To detect, prevent, and respond to fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Compliance: To meet legal obligations under applicable financial and privacy laws
  • Analytics: To understand usage patterns and improve our Service
  • Communication: To respond to your inquiries and send essential service notifications
  • Marketing: With your consent, to send product updates and announcements

Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

  • Contract: To provide services you requested
  • Legitimate Interest: To secure our platform and improve services
  • Consent: For marketing communications and optional analytics
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with applicable laws

04 Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with the following categories of third parties, only as necessary for providing the Service:

  • Blockchain Networks: Transactions are inherently public on the TRON blockchain
  • Infrastructure Providers: Vercel (hosting), Cloudflare (CDN/security), TronGrid (TRON RPC)
  • Analytics: Facebook Pixel, Google Analytics (with consent)
  • Wallet Services: WalletConnect, Trust Wallet, TronLink (for wallet connectivity)
  • Firebase (Google LLC): User data storage and authentication
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law, court order, or governmental request

All third-party processors are contractually bound to maintain the confidentiality and security of your data and process it only for specified purposes.

05 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for the following purposes:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for core platform functionality (session management, security)
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your language and display preferences
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand usage patterns (Facebook Pixel, GA)
  • LocalStorage: Stores wallet connection state and referral attribution

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may impact Service functionality.

06 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

GDPR Rights (EU/EEA Users)

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Rectification: Correct inaccurate information
  • Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request deletion of your data
  • Restriction: Limit how we process your data
  • Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format
  • Object: Opt out of certain processing activities
  • Withdraw Consent: Revoke previously given consent at any time

CCPA Rights (California Users)

  • Right to know what data we collect and how we use it
  • Right to delete your personal information
  • Right to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising privacy rights

PIPA Rights (Korean Users)

  • 개인정보 열람 요구권 (Right to access)
  • 개인정보 정정·삭제 요구권 (Right to correct or delete)
  • 개인정보 처리정지 요구권 (Right to halt processing)
  • 자동화된 결정에 대한 거부권 (Right to object to automated decisions)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via Telegram @Feeslash_support. We will respond within 30 days.

07 Data Retention

We retain your data for the following periods:

  • Active account data: As long as you continue to use our Service
  • Transaction logs: 5 years (required under Korean financial regulations)
  • Technical logs: 90 days after last activity
  • IP addresses: Anonymized after 30 days
  • Support communications: 3 years after resolution
  • Marketing data: Until you opt out or request deletion

After the retention period expires, we securely delete or anonymize your data. Note that blockchain transactions remain permanently on-chain and cannot be deleted.

08 Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data:

  • Encryption: TLS 1.3 for data in transit, AES-256 for data at rest
  • Access Controls: Role-based access with multi-factor authentication
  • Audits: Regular third-party security audits (CertiK, SlowMist)
  • Smart Contracts: Audited by reputable blockchain security firms
  • Non-Custodial Architecture: We never hold your private keys or funds
  • Incident Response: 72-hour breach notification policy (GDPR Art. 33)

While we strive to protect your data, no system is 100% secure. You are responsible for safeguarding your wallet credentials and private keys.

09 International Data Transfers

FeeSlash is operated globally. Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your residence, including the United States (Vercel, Cloudflare, Firebase), Singapore (TRON infrastructure), and the Republic of Korea.

For transfers outside the EU/EEA, we rely on:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission
  • Adequacy decisions where applicable
  • Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards
